Xesht Arbitrage

Buy a Breach unique and a Flesh of Xesht, transform the unique into its alternate, and sell it. Profit = sale price × sell share − unique price − Xesht price. Click a row for both directions of the pair.

A Foulborn unique stays Foulborn, but its Foulborn mod is re-rolled among the alternate's single-mod variants, so the outcome is random and the input's own mod doesn't matter: each row prices the cheapest one. Expected profit assumes every outcome is equally likely. Double-mod Foulborn items are left out.
